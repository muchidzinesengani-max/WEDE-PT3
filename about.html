About Mabaso Electrical Services

Our Story

Mabaso Electrical Services was founded in 2016 in Polokwane, Limpopo, by a qualified electrician with years of hands-on experience on residential and commercial sites. What began as a one-person operation has grown into a small, trusted team of electricians and an apprentice, built almost entirely on referrals from satisfied customers.

Today, we serve homeowners, landlords, and small businesses across the greater Polokwane area, handling everything from small repairs to full installations and compliance certification.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide safe, affordable, and professional electrical installation and repair services to homes and businesses in Limpopo, backed by fully compliant workmanship.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted electrical service provider in the Polokwane region, known for reliability, transparency, and technical excellence.

Qualifications & Accreditation
