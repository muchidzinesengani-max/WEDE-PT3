Electrical Services in Polokwane

We offer a full range of electrical services for residential and commercial clients across Polokwane and the surrounding Limpopo region.

Residential & Commercial Wiring

New wiring installations, rewiring of older properties, and upgrades to meet current safety standards for both homes and businesses.

Fault-Finding & Repairs

Diagnosis and repair of tripping breakers, power outages, flickering lights, and other electrical faults, usually completed on the same visit.

DB Board Upgrades

Distribution board replacements and upgrades to safely handle modern electrical loads and meet current regulations.

Certificates of Compliance (COC)

Inspection and issuing of COC certificates required for property sales, rentals, and insurance purposes.

Solar-Ready Installations

Electrical groundwork and wiring to prepare homes and businesses for solar panel and inverter installations.

Maintenance Contracts

Scheduled electrical maintenance for landlords and small businesses to prevent faults before they happen.
